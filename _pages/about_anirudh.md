**Anirudh Jakhmola**, PhD researcher

I am a PhD researcher in the [Fagan Lab](https://faganlab.com) at the University of Sheffield, funded through the MRC DiMeN Doctoral Training Partnership. I work on bacteriophages that infect _Clostridioides difficile_, combining cryo-electron microscopy, bacterial genetics and bioinformatics to study phage-host interactions.
