**Cellular Cytoskeletal Dynamics Lab, NISER Bhubaneswar · 2021–2023**<br>
Supervised by Nivedita Mitra, Dr. Ramanujam Srinivasan and Dr. Gayathri Pananghat

MreB is a bacterial actin homolog that maintains rod cell shape, and like other actins it requires ATP to polymerise. In this project, I identified potential ATP-binding sites in MreB, generated ATP-sensitive mutants at those sites, and cloned them into the fission yeast _Schizosaccharomyces pombe_ as a heterologous system. Fluorescence microscopy of the mutant filaments was used to test whether these mutations affect MreB polymerisation. The work formed part of my MS thesis, _Dynamic filament formation by bacterial actin homologs_ (NISER, 2023).
