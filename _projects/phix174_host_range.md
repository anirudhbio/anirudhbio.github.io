**Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Biology, Plön · May–Aug 2022**<br>
Supervised by Jordan Romeyer Dherbey and Dr. Frederic Bertels

The Bertels lab uses directed evolution experiments to understand how bacteriophages recognise their hosts. In this project, the phage ΦX174 was evolved on _Escherichia coli_, and the evolved phages were sequenced to isolate the mutations responsible for changes in host range.

My contributions:

- Standardised a procedure for generating site-specific mutants of ΦX174, adapting the protocol used for _E. coli_ plasmids to the phage genome
- Tested evolved ΦX174 lysates against _E. coli_ C, K-12, B and other strains to map cross-infection and host-range changes
- Troubleshot the genotypes of evolved ΦX174 phages
