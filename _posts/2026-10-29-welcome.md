Hello, and welcome!

I'm Anirudh, a PhD researcher in the [Fagan Lab](https://faganlab.com) at the University of Sheffield, where I study the bacteriophages that infect _Clostridioides difficile_. Most of my days are spent somewhere between the anaerobic cabinet, the cryo-electron microscope and a terminal window.

This blog is a place for the things that don't fit neatly into a paper. I plan to write about:

- **Phage biology** — what makes the phages of _C. difficile_ interesting, and what we are learning about how they infect and shape their hosts.
- **Methods and tools** — practical notes on cryo-EM, phage genetics and the bioinformatics pipelines I build along the way.
- **Conferences and outreach** — highlights from meetings, and from advising the Sheffield iGEM teams.
- **Life in research** — the occasional reflection on doing a PhD and what comes next.

Thanks for stopping by. If anything here sparks a question or an idea for a collaboration, feel free to [get in touch](mailto:ajakhmola1@sheffield.ac.uk).
